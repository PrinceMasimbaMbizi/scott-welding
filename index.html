Scott Welding Projects is a welding, steel fabrication and construction-focused business serving residential and commercial project needs.

We work with clients to turn their ideas and requirements into practical metalwork solutions. Whether the job involves custom gates, steel structures, carports, roofing frameworks, concrete and slab work, or repairs, we aim to deliver work that is strong, neat and suited to its intended purpose.

Every project starts with understanding what the client needs, the space available and the function the finished work must serve. We pay attention to measurements, material preparation, assembly and finishing so that the completed work is both useful and presentable.
